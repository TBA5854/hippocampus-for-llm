\section{Full experimental log: all 113 rounds}
\label{app:log}

This appendix logs every round of the search, in order, as a
(hypothesis, result) pair. It is the complete saga behind the construction in the
body: each row is a folder in the repository (\texttt{experiment/r$n$/} with its
\texttt{HYPOTHESIS.md}, \texttt{run.sh}, and \texttt{RESULTS.md}). Controls are matched
every round---store disabled, store scrambled, chain broken---and we report peak
\emph{and} final recall. A few rounds carry letter suffixes (r6b, r31d, r33b) or span a
pair (r57--r58), and three late rounds are inconclusive (r107 superseded, r109--r110 a buggy
sharding attempt); the count runs to $113$ rounds. Rounds marked $\star$ are the
milestones of \tabref{tab:milestones}.
